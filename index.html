Five empires. One common enemy.

Embers of Quinary is a top-down space action game for one player. Choose your empire, upgrade your ship, travel through jumpgates and stand against the Nullborn.

Coming soon Meet the empires

Singleplayer · Free · In development

What you can do in Embers of Quinary

A story-driven campaign, battles in large open arenas and a ship you keep building on.

Story campaign

Scripted missions, distress signals and ambushes. The Nullborn are the common enemy, and they grow more dangerous the further you get.

Upgrade your ship

Choose between ships with their own roles and weapons, and unlock more along the way.

Explore and earn

Travel between sectors through jumpgates, mine asteroids and take missions at the stations.

Play solo

No account and no internet connection needed. Save at your home station and continue later.

Five human empires

You choose your empire when you start. Each has its own history, its own ship and its own strengths.

Terran

Industrial Commonwealth

A pragmatic human civilization powered by industry, logistics, trade, mining, and immense infrastructure. Workers, corporations, armed forces, and administrators keep the galactic machine running.

Industrial logistics · Resilient infrastructure · Balanced fleets

Civilization doesn’t deliver itself.

Vyrak

Verdant Compact

A human civilization devoted to peace, prosperity, terraforming, and the patient work of making hostile worlds habitable. They believe someone must make the galaxy worth living in.

Terraforming expertise · Rapid recovery · Long-term expansion

Someone has to fix the planets.

Nerathi

Celestial Concord

Human explorers and scientists who believe the universe’s structure, anomalies, and unknown phenomena may reveal a higher intelligence. For the Nerathi, every discovery is both evidence and devotion.

Scientific discovery · High mobility · Advanced shields

Evidence is a form of worship.

Auralith

Eternal Crown

An ancient human monarchy whose royal elite possess extreme longevity or true immortality. Its beautiful, cultivated society is ruled by the same tightly controlled court for centuries at a time.

Royal continuity · Beam damage · Prismatic defenses

The throne is forever. You are replaceable.

Drakthar

War Dominion

The most militaristic of the five human civilizations. Drakthar society is built around strength, discipline, victory, and martial honor; peace is merely the interval between wars.

Heavy hulls · Military discipline · Punishing counterattacks

War is our peace treaty.

Nine mission types

From patrols and mining to the front line against the Nullborn. There is always something to do.
